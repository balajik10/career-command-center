# Evidence and contact boundaries

The application produces reviewable drafts and never sends third-party outreach or applications. Scheduled text uses at most two approved atomic claims with evidence IDs. Resume verbatim text and unapproved paraphrases are never inserted. No claim about a relationship, salary, applicant rank, or referral willingness is inferred. Channel word/character limits, unsafe claims, unresolved placeholders, and deterministic duplicate keys are validated before a draft is returned.

CSV import accepts LinkedIn Connections, Google Contacts, and generic user-owned files. It records each observed field's provenance, import time, current-company confidence, and relationship basis. A past/ambiguous employer cannot create a warm match. Imports older than 180 days need current-employment confirmation. Generic recruiting addresses are contact points with no invented person name. Public recruiting channels require explicit observed recruiting evidence on an already approved posting/page, default to outreach disabled, and expire after 30 days.

The owner must manually enable `outreach_allowed` before person-directed drafts are generated. A LinkedIn-only record receives only LinkedIn drafts. Email and phone are never guessed; local phone formats are not converted without user-supplied country information. WhatsApp additionally requires `whatsapp_allowed` and phone-field provenance; a public personal number never implies permission. A user-owned current-company relationship is WARM_CONNECTION until a recorded agreement makes it CONFIRMED_REFERRER. Public strangers receive a role inquiry, not an implied referral ask.

Draft keys include immutable job ID, contact ID, channel, purpose, and material-change sequence. The outreach ledger suppresses repeated drafts. A person has a 30-day cross-job cooldown after a recorded send; bypass requires an explicit reason. A single follow-up is eligible after five business days only when no reply and no opt-out are recorded. No follow-up send is automatic.

With no verified contact, the brief contains manual search phrases and a checklist only; the software never executes person-search queries. A cover note for the verified application form can still be prepared using approved evidence. Apply promptly and prepare any real referral in parallel; unknown referral policies permit waiting at most half the application SLA.

Redaction replaces identifying fields with a domain-separated HMAC suppression token and opt-out marker. The same private key prevents reimport of the same observed identity without retaining its original value. Never rotate or discard the HMAC key without migrating suppression tokens from authorized private records. Public-derived expired contacts can be purged; user contacts are never automatically expired.
