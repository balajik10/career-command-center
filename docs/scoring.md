# Deterministic scoring

`config/scoring.yaml` records versioned fit weights, operational weights, mappings, thresholds, penalties, dedupe thresholds, and SLA defaults. `ScoringConfig` accepts private Sheet overrides; positive dimension weights must total 100. Private current base, target base, and company-tier values belong in Profile, never this repository. The public profile and every example are synthetic.

Runtime configuration currently accepts the private Sheet `Config` rows `scoring` and `data_quality`. The public role taxonomy and skill alias YAML files document the versioned defaults; v1 does not dynamically reload these two files from Sheet rows. Updating title classification or skill aliases requires a reviewed code/config change with matching tests. Dedupe thresholds can be supplied through the typed `DedupeConfig` API but are not yet loaded from the private Sheet by the scan command.

The parse-yield guard compares a complete parsed response from an official inventory or permitted RSS/Atom feed with the median of successful complete responses from the preceding fourteen days. It flags a strictly greater-than-70% drop once at least three samples and a median of ten jobs exist; small or insufficient samples retain the ordinary repeated-miss safeguards. These minimum sample/volume thresholds avoid treating a one-job board's genuine closure as a mass-loss event. Configure `drop_threshold`, `min_completed_samples`, and `min_baseline_jobs` in the private `data_quality` JSON row. A flagged snapshot keeps independently valid observations, records source counts in Quarantine, sets DEGRADED subject to health precedence, and advances neither the success watermark nor closure counters. Restored complete responses clear the anomaly counter. RSS, email, and manual imports are never treated as complete inventories for source disappearance, even though feed yield can be monitored for anomalies.

Fit comprises role 20, experience 15, must-have skills 25, matched evidence 15, location 10, responsibilities 10, and education 5. Required skill coverage receives 90% of the skills dimension when preferred skills are also present; preferred coverage receives 10%. With no stated skills, coverage is explicitly uncertain at 50%. Exact aliases such as PostgreSQL/Postgres match; related technologies never invent skills. Evidence selection ranks explicit tags and technologies and returns at most two evidence IDs.

Full-time dates retain YYYY-MM precision. Tenure reports minimum and maximum possible years from the last and first instant of the starting month. Internship duration is calculated separately and added only when the posting explicitly counts internships. A requirement inside the tenure interval is AMBIGUOUS. Two-year requirements remain NEAR_MATCH_IN_SCOPE until the lower tenure bound proves them met. Three-year roles require fit >=75 and no senior scope; four-year roles are ordinarily excluded. Preferred and technology-specific years do not become total required experience.

Priority is fit 55, freshness 15, experience 8, official route 7, private target tier 5, compensation 4, legitimate contact readiness 4, and deadline 2. Compensation compares only observed annual INR base salary against private profile values, in rupees/100000; total compensation and foreign currencies remain unknown. Unknown compensation earns half the available points. No compensation or contact value is a hard exclusion.

Numeric S0/S1/S2/S3 boundaries are 85, 70, and 50. The ordered action table is independent: existing application -> PIPELINE; verified hard constraint -> EXCLUDED; fit >=75, priority >=85, verified route, fresh age <24h -> P0; fit >=60, priority >=70, verified route, age <192h -> P1; remaining score >=50 -> P2; remaining -> P3. Quarantine suppresses P0/P1 regardless of numeric score. A date-unknown initial baseline cannot pass the observed-new gate. Post-baseline observed-new can qualify; material changes can qualify for P1. Manual numeric priority overrides preserve these gates.

A synthetic full-fit role with a verified route, no contact, unknown salary, and a source posting age below six hours scores 87 (P0). The same role posted two days ago scores 81.75 (P1). A date-unknown baseline scores 72 but stays P2 because its age is unknown. All source age intervals use their oldest plausible age for boundaries. Missing publication is never filled from an updated timestamp. User-facing times have an explicit IST suffix.

Canonical job UIDs survive promotion to official sources. IDs compare only within provider/tenant or issuer-defined requisition namespaces. Conservative fuzzy matching additionally requires identical normalized legal employer, matching observed location/work mode, complete title/description evidence, configured similarity thresholds, and a 45-day window. DO_NOT_MERGE blocks cross-record merging. Two complete misses at least twelve hours apart imply possibly closed; a third implies closed. Partial/failed runs do not advance missing counters. Reappearance preserves the original first-seen timestamp.
